    Our Craft

    Katan
    Pure silk woven from twisted silk threads — the classic base of the Banarasi saree, with a rich fall and lasting sheen.
    Zari
    Fine metallic thread in gold or silver tones, woven into the borders, pallu and motifs.
    Meenakari
    Coloured silk threads worked into zari motifs, like enamel set in gold jewellery.
    Jangla
    An all-over trellis of flowering vines — one of the oldest and most loved Banarasi patterns.
    Booti & Boota
    Small (booti) and larger (boota) woven motifs scattered across the body of the saree.
    Rangkat
    A weave that blends several colours in one saree, for a painterly, multi-toned look.
